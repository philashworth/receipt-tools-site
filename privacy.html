Privacy policy

Gmail Receipt Extractor is used only by its owner, with their own Google account.

  • It sends receipt scans from the owner's Gmail to the same address, and reads and files those emails.
  • Receipt details are read using OpenAI's API and saved on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
